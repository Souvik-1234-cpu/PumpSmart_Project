## 5. Physics Visualization Summary (Per Group)

### Group A — Single Faults ✅ Physically Valid

Each fault shows the correct primary channel response after t=50 onset, with all
other channels near baseline. Key observations:

- **Bearing wear (label 1):** Mot.SV* monotonic exponential rise (Paris-Erdogan
  `da/dN = C·ΔK^m`, C=1.8×10⁻⁴, m=2.8). Thermal lag 20-40 steps before
  Mot.TV* and Temp.SV* follow (r=0.9793 coupling confirmed). MAE crosses
  threshold progressively — no instantaneous jump.

- **Impeller imbalance (label 2):** Pmp.SV* amplitude-modulated envelope
  (ISO 1940 unbalance `F = m·e·ω²` at 2980 RPM). Oscillating character,
  not monotonic — correct for periodic forcing at BPF=347.67 Hz.

- **Cavitation (label 3):** Pres.SV* drops sharply with chaotic noise (NPSHa < NPSHr,
  bubble nucleation and collapse). 150 steps — acute fault, short sequence justified.
  Pmp.SV* kurtosis elevated (bubble impingement signature).

- **Seal failure (label 4):** Pres.SV* smooth monotonic negative drift over 400 steps —
  orifice equation `Q_leak = Cd·A·√(2ΔP/ρ)` at 40 bar. Gradual, not sudden.
  Correct for a mechanical seal developing a leak. Contrast with label 19 below.

- **Overloading (label 5):** Temp.SV* rising slope (dT*/dt > 0) — `Cp·m·dT/dt > rated
  heat dissipation`. Rate-of-change pattern, not absolute level. Other channels
  stable — overloading is initially a thermal phenomenon at this pump.

- **Sensor failure (label 6):** Mot.SV* → 0 (flatline, cable cut). All 7 other channels
  completely normal. Single-channel std collapse is the detection signature.

- **Seal failure fast (label 19):** ⚠️ Representative sequence shows gradual character
  rather than the expected ≤20-step collapse. This may be a high-severity edge case
  selected as the representative. Per physics spec: turbulent orifice blowout
  `Q = Cd·A·√(2ΔP/ρ)` should collapse Pres.SV* in ≤20 steps (NOT Hagen-Poiseuille).
  **Monitor label 19 F1 in M7 — target >0.80.**

## 6. WARN Gate Physical Justification

### 6.1 Gate D3 — Multi-Sensor Count (47.2%, target ≥90%)

**Root cause:** `multi_sensor_anomaly_count = (mae_w > 0.15).sum()` fires only during
spike windows for label 22 (intermittent character). The 0.15 threshold is appropriate
for Group A-D faults but too tight for label 22's mild spike MAE.

**Impact:** Feature still discriminative for M7 (Fisher 1.33). Group E classification
falls back on `masked_channel_flag` (100%) and `fault_group_id`.

**Correction possible?** Yes — lower threshold to 0.08. **Decision: DO NOT CORRECT.**
Lowering would increase false positives on other classes. The feature is working as
designed for all other groups. Group E is covered by other features.

### 6.2 Gate D5 — Label 21 Slope (68.7%, target ≥95%)

**Root cause:** Paris law at severity 0.05–0.15 produces slope ≈ 0.0001–0.0003/step
over a 50-step window. With SCADA noise std ≈ 0.015, slope SNR < 1 for early windows.
The 50-step `err_slope_MotSV` cannot resolve Paris law signal below noise floor at
low severity — this is expected physics.

**Impact:** ZERO impact on CUSUM detection. `score_B` (OLS slope of z_t_recon_err
over full sequence) = positive in 99.4% of label 21 windows. The sequence-level
z_t trajectory resolves what the per-window statistic cannot.

**Correction possible?** Only by increasing minimum severity in M6B label 21 generation,
which would destroy the sub-threshold physics that is the liability class's entire purpose.
**Decision: DO NOT CORRECT. WARN is accepted.**

### 6.3 Gate Z2 — score_C Group B (72.5%, target ≥80%)

**Root cause:** z_t pkl files use T//50 (non-overlapping) windowing = 4–18 z_t points
per sequence. Max-delta (score_C) computed over only 4–18 differences has lower
statistical power than it would with stride-25 windowing (which would give 2× more points).

**Impact:** `onset_order` (Fisher rank 1 = 9.27×10¹³) dominates compound classification
in M7. score_C contributes additively, not as the sole discriminator.

**Correction possible?** Only by re-exporting z_t from M6B with stride-25 → requires
re-running M6B (LOCKED module). **Decision: DO NOT CORRECT.**

**M7 action:** If Group B F1 < 0.72, revisit score_C formula (mean Δ vs max Δ).

### 6.4 Gate F1 — Fisher Score (13 features < 0.5)

**Root cause:** Fisher score measures univariate between-class vs within-class variance
ratio. Multi-severity continuous features (slopes, MAE values) have high within-class
variance by design — severity ranges from 0.05 to 0.9 across sequences.

**13 flagged features:**
`std_err_MotSV` (0.099), `err_slope_MotSV` (0.056), `err_slope_TempSV` (0.025),
`err_slope_PresSV` (0.049), `variant_slope_ratio` (0.028), `cross_channel_MotSV_PmpSV`
(0.297), `kurtosis_PmpSV` (0.229), `mae_MotSV` (0.324), `mae_PmpSV` (0.429),
`max_err_all` (0.475), `mae_MotTV` (0.209), `z_t_recon_err` (0.463), `mae_PresSV` (0.474)

**Assessment:** These are the physically essential features. The slope features
(0.025–0.056) are the ONLY features that carry temporal rate-of-change information.
XGBoost ensemble trees split on combinations of these features, gaining discriminability
that univariate Fisher cannot measure. **Decision: RETAIN ALL 33 FEATURES.**

## 9. Model Limitation Disclaimer (M10 Propagation Required)

The feature matrix was generated from CIRA-anchored physics-synthetic data for a
**110 kW, 7-stage centrifugal pump at 2980 RPM, 40 bar, 45 m³/h**. The following
real-world conditions are NOT captured in any synthetic sequence and must be surfaced
in every M10 API response:

- Corrosion state of impellers (not in sensor data)
- Fluid contamination or abrasive particulates
- Upstream/downstream pump interaction (single-pump model)
- Ambient temperature extremes beyond M3 cluster baseline range
- Sensor placement variation from CIRA installation geometry
- Confidence scores may be lower on real-world faults than on simulated training data
